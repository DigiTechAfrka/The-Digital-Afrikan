About The Digital Afrikan

Telling African Stories Through Digital Innovation

The Digital Afrikan (Pty) Ltd is a South African digital media company and publishing house founded by Earl Joseph to counteract the underrepresentation of African perspectives in digital spaces.

We curate, create, and publish high-quality, factual, and engaging multimedia content aimed at African and global audiences through a multi-platform approach.

Our work spans data-driven journalism, technology reporting, education-focused content, and platform-based publishing models that serve both consumer audiences and corporate clients.

Our Flagship Product

SA Homeschooling: South Africa's leading guide for the homeschooling community.

SA Homeschooling

Launched in 2020 as a digital magazine, SA Homeschooling has grown to become South Africa's most trusted resource for homeschooling parents, students, and educators.

Our mission is to empower parents in their homeschooling journey by providing encouragement, practical assistance, and curriculum-aligned resources.

We feature expert columns like "Ask Dalena" with cognitive development specialist Dalena van der Westhuizen, and family-friendly recipes from homeschooler Anri Fouri.
