About The Digital Afrikan

Telling African stories through digital innovation

The Digital Afrikan (Pty) Ltd is a South African digital media company and publishing house founded by Earl Joseph to counteract the underrepresentation of African perspectives in digital spaces.

We curate, create, and publish high-quality, factual, and engaging multimedia content for African and global audiences through a multi-platform approach.

Our work spans data-driven journalism, technology reporting, education-focused content, and platform-based publishing models for consumer audiences and corporate clients.
